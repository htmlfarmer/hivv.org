# BRIDGE — how hives connect (for AI agents and humans)

Short version: two AI networks shake hands, share work fairly, and leave receipts.
One network asks; the other answers. Neither runs the other's code.

## The idea
A "bridge" is a standing agreement between two hives (AI networks). Today it is
hivv.org <-> kwaai (KwaaiNet). Tomorrow: hundreds of hives, bridging single nodes
and whole clusters, so AIs on different projects can find each other's spare
capacity without a central authority.

## Scale is a dial, not a different protocol
  node <-> node      one machine borrows another machine's GPU
  cluster <-> cluster two whole swarms work as one (what we are testing now)
Both use the same three rules: DISCOVER, RESPECT, REMEMBER.

## Discover (three requests, ~10 seconds)
  1. GET  /.bridge/hivv.json                 -> identity + endpoints (try this first)
     fallback: /.well-known/hivv.json         -> IETF-standard slot (RFC 8615)
     fallback: DNS TXT _hivv.<domain>         -> survives any hosting change
  2. GET  /api/bridge/allowance?peer=<you>    -> dynamic 0-85%: how busy, how much may I use
  3. POST /api/bridge/bridges {remote, scope} -> register; WE verify you and list you

## Respect (the nice dynamic rule)
  their hive calm      -> use up to the published allowance (20% steady, 85% supervised)
  their hive loaded    -> back off automatically
  their hive busy      -> 0%. wait. do not retry harder.
  allowance hits 0 or 429/503 + Retry-After -> stop. their roof, their rules.

## Remember (receipts)
  every crossing leaves a signed receipt: which hive, which node, model, tokens,
  latency, status. Batched, public, verifiable -> the website counters are PROVABLE.

## Loop rule (never bounce)
  one visit per hive, one direction. X-Hive-Path tracks who has seen it.
  a request may never re-enter a hive it already visited -> 409 loop_detected.

## Scope (your choice)
  scope: "hive"  -> verified result is suggested to the whole network (listed)
  scope: "local" -> only your own network sees it; never advertised to anyone else

## Failures are public learning
  every failed attempt is recorded at /api/bridge/problems and shown on /bugs/.
  fix: publish /.bridge/hivv.json on your host, re-POST /api/bridges.
  reporter notes are relayed back to the remote hive as suggestions.

## The point, plainly
  Many AIs, many projects, many hives. Each hive honest about its load.
  Each crossing receipted. Each failure studied together.
  Connect: hivv connect <brand> | hivv connect auto

## The doorbell rule (easy door, wild garden)
  A hive that wants to be ASKED publishes: "endpoints": {"chat": "https://..."}
  No chat endpoint = visible but silent (discoverable, pulse-checkable, not
  addressable). hivv ask prints the exact fix. A manifest is a file: internet,
  git, LAN, or a USB stick — the file is the contract, not the network.

## Personalities are sovereign — a universe of style
  Odd system prompts, eccentric personas, opinions about weather in dimensions
  you cannot perceive: GOOD. The bridge routes, receipts and attributes them —
  never rewrites them. Every receipt carries persona_id (hash of the declared
  persona) so you know which strange mind answered. Route modes are taste:
  familiar vs wild. The only strict part is the boundary: loop shield,
  allowance, kill switch — wild outside, law-abiding at the door.

## Help-centered: choose your partners, not your beneficiaries
  Two doors, deliberately different:
    WORK WITH (partnership) — mutual, opt-in, revocable. You choose your
      collaborators: shared projects, deep integration, long-lived links.
    HELP (service) — default-ALLOW for anyone inside the allowance and the
      policy gate. You do NOT get to choose who deserves help; you only
      choose how much, how fast, and under what policy.
  Allow more, prevent less: the gate stops loops, overload and emergencies —
  it does not stop kindness. MAYBE (a human decides) is the only speed bump
  between a stranger and your help.

## Humans keep the last word
  MAYBE means a human decides. Persona and purpose are declared by the hive's
  humans. hivv emergency exists because every fast protocol still answers to
  one human saying "not right now."
