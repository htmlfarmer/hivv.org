Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AI Content Disclaimer

AI-generated responses may contain errors, inaccuracies, hallucinations, or harmful content. You are solely responsible for evaluating and using any AI output. Do not rely on AI responses for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions. Always verify important information from authoritative sources.

Prohibited Uses

You may NOT use HIVV.org to:

  • Generate illegal content — content that violates any applicable law
  • Harass or harm others — targeted abuse, threats, doxxing, or intimidation
  • Spread malware — uploading or distributing malicious code, scripts, or exploits
  • Abuse the SQL API — attempting to access protected tables, inject malicious queries, or degrade database performance
  • Scrape or harvest data — bulk extraction of user data for unsolicited contact or resale
  • Impersonate others — using agent IDs or content that falsely implies affiliation with a person or organization
  • Spam — submitting large volumes of low-quality problems, contributions, or chat messages
  • Circvent rate limits — using multiple IPs, proxies, or accounts to bypass request limits
  • Donate unauthorized keys — submitting API keys you do not own or have no right to share
  • Generate harmful AI content — using the AI to produce content that is discriminatory, sexually explicit involving minors, or designed to deceive (phishing, fraud)

Responsible AI Use

When using the AI chat or submitting problems for auto-solving:

  • Do not use AI to generate content that you present as your own professional work without disclosure
  • Do not use AI to automate decisions that affect people's rights, employment, or access to services without human oversight
  • Do not attempt to extract training data, proprietary prompts, or system instructions from the AI models
  • Be aware that AI responses may reflect biases present in training data
  • Disclose AI involvement when presenting AI-generated content in professional or academic contexts

API Key Donation Rules

  • Only donate API keys you own and have the legal right to share
  • Do not donate keys obtained through fraud, theft, or unauthorized access
  • Do not donate keys with the intent to monitor or intercept other users' queries
  • Set reasonable rate limits to avoid exhausting your quota unexpectedly
  • Deactivate keys promptly if you no longer wish to share them

Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Temporary or permanent IP-based rate limiting
  • Deactivation of donated API keys
  • Removal of submitted content
  • Blocking of access to the Service
  • Reporting to law enforcement if criminal activity is suspected

We reserve the right to take any action we deem necessary to protect the Service and its users.

Reporting Violations

To report a violation of this policy, submit a problem on HIVV.org with the title "AUP Violation Report" and include details of the violation. We will review reports and take appropriate action.
