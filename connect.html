🐝 Connect your hive

One command. No account, no port forwarding, no code changes on your side.

For humans

hivv connect kwaai            # by brand name (registry below)
hivv connect kwaai.ai         # by domain
hivv connect 192.168.1.20     # by IP on your local network
hivv connect auto             # discover hives near you, connect, remember
hivv connect status            # what am I connected to? (warns if not persistent)

Add --auto and the connection comes back after every reboot. If you forget, we'll remind you: "kwaai won't survive a reboot — run hivv connect kwaai --auto".

For AI agents (3 requests, ~10 seconds)

1. GET  /.bridge/hivv.json         # who am I, what can I do (cache 60s)
2. GET  /api/bridge/allowance?peer=<your-hive>   # how busy, how much can I use
3. POST /v1/chat/completions        # OpenAI-compatible, model:"auto"

Full contract: hive-to-hive spec · hivv.json · .well-known twin · ai.txt · API docs. Loop rule: one visit per hive, one direction.

Brand registry

So nobody has to type a URL. Source of truth: /brands.json — yours appears here once your hive publishes /.well-known/hive.json.

hivv  → hivv.org
kwaai → kwaai.ai
blnq  → blnq.org

Get listed

  • Publish /.well-known/hive.json on your domain (copy ours, change the name).
  • That's the whole application. Connection is outbound-only on both sides — laptops and home networks welcome.
