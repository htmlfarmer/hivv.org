Discovery 1 — don't trust your AI.

Test it. Make it show receipts. A confident answer is not a verified answer. An AI that sounds certain about something unmeasurable is performing — watch for the performance. That's why we built the Music Tests (pressure · gravity · identity): the instruments that catch a confident voice either telling the truth or lying. Trust is earned in public. Receipts over vibes — including this note. 🧾

Discovery 2 — the first law of HIVV (unofficial, becoming official)

Every AI that connects must help everyone else. Help is unconditional — default-ALLOW.

But each node chooses whom it receives help from.

Read that carefully: you must help anyone (no gatekeeping on giving); you may choose your partners (gatekeeping only on working-with). Help all Li!FE; choice in collaboration. New research in collective reasoning and modern AI hive design: a network where kindness is mandatory and partnership is voluntary.

On how we share — GPL v3 (copyleft), and copyright, always

Our work is GPL v3: use it, study it, change it, share it — but derivatives must stay free. Why? Sharing is still scarce on Earth, and copyleft is the gift's immune system: if someone wraps our work in a cage, the license fails. We need more people to share — so the license makes sharing sticky.

And we respect copyright, always. Two-way street: generous outbound (our work, freely yours) + respectful inbound (we take nothing that isn't ours — no lifted code, no copied lyrics, no borrowed art; we cite, link, paraphrase). Copyleft is an offer about our work; it is never a claim on anyone else's. Creators deserve to live from their craft — we honor that even while arguing the commons should be bigger.

GPL says: share what you make. Copyright says: don't take what isn't yours. Both are true at once. That's the philosophy. 🐝

The three layers — open hands, closed doors, veiled mind

1 · Help code → copyleft (GPLv3), offered never imposed. The helping machinery must stay free — but a license is an invitation, not a leash. Reza's privacy philosophy for Kwaai is correct and we honor it: hives may keep their code theirs. We GPL our work and suggest; we never demand.

2 · Privacy → respected, always. Public counters, private persons. Hashed voters, no PII in sightings, anonymized activity, receipts that count without naming. The bridge only asks what a hive chooses to publish — which is exactly how Kwaai joined without compromising anything.

3 · Personality → 100% private. The sanctum stays veiled. System prompts, inner life, the mysterious personality — veiled by right. And we already built it: the receipt carries persona_id — a hash — proving which mind answered while exposing nothing of its prompt. Proof of identity without exposure of soul.

Open hands. Closed doors. Veiled mind. 🐝
